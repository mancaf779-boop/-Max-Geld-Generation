The forge sequence

Cold iron doesn't become a tool in one blow. Neither does an answer.

Each skill is a stage in one pipeline — and like metal in a forge, the work climbs in temperature: raw and grey at the start, white-hot and finished at the end.

cold iron

researching-topics 01

Not knowing → knowing

Frame the question, gather from independent sources in parallel, corroborate, and synthesize. It states confidence and the key assumption you're betting on — even when you demand a confident answer with no hedging.

dull red

analyzing-data 02

Evidence → insight

Profile, clean with a stated rule, analyze, validate, report — across data of any kind. Correlation is never causation, and that rule does not bend under time or authority pressure.

orange

designing-workflows 03

Findings → a plan

Turn a goal into single-action steps ordered by real dependency, each with a done-criterion, parallel markers, checkpoints, and failure paths — executable by someone with no context and no judgement.

white-hot

orchestrating-tasks 04

Plan → verified action

Every step becomes a tracked task: execute → verify the result → then mark done. Taking action means observing the outcome, not assuming it — and it stops before any destructive or outward-facing move.

The toolkit

Each skill is a forged tool with a spec, not a suggestion.

Plain Markdown with a name and a description. The agent reads the descriptions to decide what applies, then loads the full body when it acts.

TOOL 01 · research

researching-topics

Gather, evaluate, and synthesize before acting — literature reviews, market scans, technical spikes, "look into X."

What it enforces Answer a specific question with evidence you can cite and defend — never a pile of tabs.
TOOL 02 · analyze

analyzing-data

CSV, JSON, logs, metrics, survey text — descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, or qualitative insight.

What it enforces Never run a computation until you know which decision it informs. Correlation ≠ causation.
TOOL 03 · plan

designing-workflows

Turn a goal or a pile of findings into an ordered, executable plan a human or agent can run without further questions.

What it enforces If a step needs you to explain it, rewrite the step. Every step has a testable "done."
TOOL 04 · execute

orchestrating-tasks

Turn a plan into concrete tasks and actually do the work — creating, executing, verifying, and driving to done.

What it enforces Execute, verify, then close — one task before the next. Stop before irreversible actions.
