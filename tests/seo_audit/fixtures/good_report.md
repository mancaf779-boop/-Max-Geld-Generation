**Client:** Example Client
**Website:** `example.com`
**Prepared by:** Claude

**Report type:** Mock audit

> **Data note:** All datasets used in this mock report are synthetic.

## Executive Summary

**Organic traffic grew steadily over the last six months, driven mainly by non-branded blog content.** The data shows organic clicks increased from ten thousand to over twelve thousand five hundred across the measured window, with the largest single contribution coming from long-form guides in the blog section. Branded search still accounts for over half of all traffic, which signals continued brand-dependency risk even as non-branded discovery keeps expanding. Keyword concentration is moderate: the top ten keywords represent a meaningful but not dominant share of total clicks, leaving room to diversify further. On the technical side, the most important finding is a site architecture issue where several commercial pages sit four or more clicks from the homepage, diluting crawl budget and internal link equity. The backlink profile shows active link building over the period, with referring domains growing steadily and a healthy mix of branded and non-branded anchor text pointing into feature pages rather than only the homepage, suggesting an SEO-team-led rather than purely PR-led approach.

## Organic Traffic Trend

Organic traffic grew across the period.

## Final Prioritization

1. **Problem:** Commercial pages sit too deep in the site architecture.
   **Fix:** Reduce click depth for priority commercial pages to two clicks or fewer.
